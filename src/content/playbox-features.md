> Author it on your desk. Fill it with people. Run it live. Playbox is one surface for the whole arc — the tool that designs the experience is the same tool that runs it.

## Author the world
The view is the edit. There's no separate build mode — you shape the world while you're standing in it, and the people in it with you shape it back.
- Describe what you want in plain language; watch it appear
- Compose spaces from parts that snap together
- Co-authorship is the default, not a multiplayer add-on
- Productive in minutes, not weeks

## Bring real places in
Capture a real space and walk through it — photorealistic, fast, no manual modelling. The look that tells everyone this is real.
- Turn a phone capture into a navigable space
- Real rooms carry their own light and depth
- Set the scene, then author the moment on top of it

## Living media, not files
Everything coming into and out of your world — camera, video, audio, sensors — is a live part of the scene. A world is the thing happening in it; it doesn't go to disk and come back.
- Drop a camera and every viewer sees it instantly
- Feeds and inputs are first-class scene material
- The world lives, responds, and remembers

## The audience is in it
Presence, movement, and gesture become inputs. Viewer becomes participant becomes player — and what someone adds on night one is still there on night fifty.
- The crowd's energy shapes the space in real time
- A sub-125ms loop: walk in and you're already part of the show
- The experience keeps a memory of everyone who was there

## Runs everywhere, owned by you
Native on Windows and macOS, on commodity hardware. Your world is your own — self-host it, move it, hand it off, or archive it.
- Runs on a $1,500 mini PC
- Open and interoperable — no vendor lock-in
- Your world is your own database, in your own region

## A team of agents
Agents work alongside you as real collaborators — they hold roles, build in parallel, and do the work that scales so you can do the work that doesn't.
- Agents build and respond in the world like people do
- Scale a production with agents, not headcount
- You direct; the fleet executes
